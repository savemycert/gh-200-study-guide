# GH-200 Exam-Day Guide

This page covers the logistics of the GitHub Actions (GH-200) exam: booking, delivery options, check-in, exam format, scoring, results, retakes and keeping the certification current. GH-200 is delivered through Microsoft Learn and Pearson VUE, so most policies are Microsoft's. Every fact links to the official Microsoft Learn page it came from. Policies change, so confirm anything time-sensitive on the linked page before your appointment.

> Also see the [GH-200 practice questions](https://www.savemycert.com/practice/github-actions/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=readme&utm_campaign=gh-200-study-guide) and [timed mock exams](https://www.savemycert.com/mocks/github-actions/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=readme&utm_campaign=gh-200-study-guide).

## Booking the exam

On the GitHub Actions certification page, choose **Schedule exam** under Pearson VUE. You may be asked to sign in to (or create) your Learn profile. Microsoft strongly recommends a personal Microsoft account (MSA): if you register with a work or school account, your exam records are lost if you leave that organization. If your Learn profile has no legal name, you are asked to add one, and it must match your ID or you cannot take the exam. You then enter your address, phone number and contact email, and finish scheduling on the Pearson VUE site.

You can schedule no more than 90 days in advance, and you can hold at most two Microsoft Certification exam appointments at a time through Pearson VUE.

The price depends on the country or region where the exam is proctored, so check the certification page for your local price. GH-200 is offered in English, Spanish, Portuguese (Brazil), Korean and Japanese. If the exam is not available in your preferred language, the study guide says you can request an additional 30 minutes.

Source: [GitHub Actions certification](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/credentials/certifications/github-actions/), [Register and schedule an exam](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/credentials/certifications/register-schedule-exam), [Study guide for Exam GH-200](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/credentials/certifications/resources/study-guides/gh-200)

## Rescheduling and canceling

Reschedule or cancel at least 24 hours before your appointment, or your fee is forfeited (a company-purchased voucher is forfeited too). You do this from your Learn profile: open Credentials, then Certifications, find the appointment and choose Reschedule or Cancel, which takes you to Pearson VUE.

Source: [Reschedule and cancellation policies](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/credentials/support/reschedule-and-cancellation-policy)

## Online (OnVUE) vs test center

In most cases you can test online or at a Pearson VUE test center. If you don't see an online option, it isn't available from your exam provider. A test center is a quiet, pre-configured room with no software to install. Online, you are responsible for your computer and room meeting the security rules:

- Run the Pearson VUE system test on the same computer, network and location before you register, and again on exam day. Prefer a personal computer; work computers often block the OnVUE software. Your login account needs local administrator rights.
- Use a walled room with a closed door. No one else may be in or enter the room during the exam.
- Unplug extra monitors and turn off other computers. Keep books, notes, papers and pens out of arm's reach.
- Keep your phone out of arm's reach but close enough to hear, in case the proctor calls. A drink is allowed in a transparent, spill-proof container.
- Greeters and proctors communicate in English (with limited Japanese availability), even if your exam is in another language.
- You are recorded on video and audio, and you may not get help from anyone or from any AI tool.

Source: [Register and schedule an exam](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/credentials/certifications/register-schedule-exam), [About online exams with Pearson VUE](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/credentials/certifications/online-exams)

## ID and check-in

You need a current government-issued ID, and the name on it must match the name on your Microsoft Certification profile exactly. Legal name-change forms and ID renewal forms are not accepted for online exams. Pearson VUE's ID policy lists acceptable documents.

For an online exam, check in from your Learn profile (Credentials, then Certifications, then **Go to the exam**) between 30 minutes before and 15 minutes after your appointment time. You photograph your face, your ID and your exam area with your phone, and a greeter reviews them before placing you in the proctor queue. The launch and check-in process takes about 15 minutes.

Source: [About online exams with Pearson VUE](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/credentials/certifications/online-exams)

## How the exam is structured

You have 100 minutes to complete the exam, and it is proctored. The certification page says you may have interactive components to complete. Microsoft does not publish a question count for GH-200; its general guidance is that most Microsoft Certification exams contain between 40 and 60 questions, but the number varies by exam.

Microsoft does not say in advance which question types an exam uses. The free [GH-200 exam sandbox](https://aka.ms/GHExamDemo-enu) lets you try the exam interface and question types before test day. Unlike Microsoft's associate role-based exams, GitHub exams do **not** give you access to Microsoft Learn during the test.

Five minutes are built in for unscheduled breaks. The clock keeps running during a break, and afterward you cannot return to any question you saw before it, even unanswered or marked ones.

The skills measured (as of January 2026) are Author and manage workflows (20–25%), Consume and troubleshoot workflows (15–20%), Author and maintain actions (15–20%), Manage GitHub Actions for the enterprise (20–25%) and Secure and optimize automation (10–15%). Most questions cover generally available features, but the exam may include preview features if they are commonly used.

Source: [GitHub Actions certification](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/credentials/certifications/github-actions/), [Exam duration and exam experience](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/credentials/support/exam-duration-exam-experience), [Study guide for Exam GH-200](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/credentials/certifications/resources/study-guides/gh-200)

## How scoring works

Scores are reported on a scale of 1 to 1,000, and you need 700 or more to pass. Because the score is scaled, 700 does not necessarily mean 70% of the points.

Most multi-part questions give one point per correct part, so partial credit is possible. There is no penalty for guessing. Some questions are unscored, and you cannot tell which ones, so answer every question as if it counts.

Source: [Exam scoring and score reports](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/credentials/certifications/exam-scoring-reports), [Study guide for Exam GH-200](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/credentials/certifications/resources/study-guides/gh-200)

## Results and score report

For most exams, you get your result within minutes of finishing, and your score appears in your Learn profile within 24 hours. The score report shows your overall score, pass or fail, and a bar chart of your performance in each skill area. The bars show relative strengths and weaknesses, but you cannot use them to work out how many questions you got right. Microsoft does not tell you which questions you missed.

Source: [Exam scoring and score reports](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/credentials/certifications/exam-scoring-reports)

## Keeping the certification current

The certification page states that GitHub certifications are valid for 2 years. GitHub is moving to Microsoft's recertification process, which will let you maintain a certification without retaking the full exam. During the transition, GitHub certifications that expire before the new process is available are extended by 6 months. This is changing, so check the certification page for the current position.

Source: [GitHub Actions certification](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/credentials/certifications/github-actions/)

## If you don't pass

- After a first failed attempt, wait 24 hours before retaking.
- After that, wait 14 days between attempts.
- You can take the same exam at most five times in 12 months, counted from your first attempt.
- You pay for each retake (if applicable).
- A waiver of the waiting period is granted only for internet connectivity issues or equipment failure during the exam, with a Pearson VUE case number.

Source: [Exam retake policy](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/credentials/support/retake-policy), [GitHub Actions certification](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/credentials/certifications/github-actions/)

## On exam day: practical tips

- **Try the sandbox first.** It uses the real exam interface and navigation.
- **Read the YAML slowly.** Many GH-200 questions turn on one key: where a context is allowed, which trigger only reads the default branch, or which scope a `permissions` block leaves at none.
- **Answer before a break.** Anything you have seen before a break is locked afterward.
- **Never leave a blank.** A wrong answer costs nothing.
- **No lookups.** Microsoft Learn is not available during GitHub exams, so know the exact syntax.
